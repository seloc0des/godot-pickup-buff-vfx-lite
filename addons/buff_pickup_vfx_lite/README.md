# Pickup & Buff VFX Lite (Godot 4)

A free, fully working sample from **Pickup, Buff & Heal VFX**: one multi-layered
**level-up burst**, complete with source. Open this project and press **Play**,
or copy `addons/buff_pickup_vfx_lite/` into your project and drag
`scenes/buff_levelup.tscn` into a scene. It plays in the editor and in game.

The exact system the full pack uses: a soft volume, the hero shape, a pulse
ring, rising textured sparks and a real-time light, HDR-tuned for glow. Three
colours in the preset turn the level-up gold into a heal green or mana cyan.

## Install
Copy `addons/buff_pickup_vfx_lite/` into your project's `res://addons/`.

## Requirements
Godot 4.x (tested on 4.7), Forward Plus recommended, glow enabled. Copy the
environment from `demo/dragdrop_example.tscn`.
